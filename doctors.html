✦ Clinical Team

About Our Clinicians

Dr. Maya Chen, DDS

General Dentist & Practice Lead

Dr. Chen provides comprehensive general and family dentistry with an emphasis on prevention, restorative care, and clear treatment conversations. She believes patients should understand their options before deciding on care.

  • Preventive dentistry
  • Family dentistry & restorative care
  • Treatment planning & patient education

Dr. Ethan Patel, DMD

General & Restorative Dentist

Dr. Patel focuses on restoring damaged teeth and helping patients understand practical options for maintaining function and comfort.

  • Restorative dentistry
  • Crowns, bridges & root canal treatment
  • Comprehensive examinations

Dr. Olivia Nguyen, DMD

Family & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Nguyen combines family dental care with cosmetic consultations for patients interested in improving the appearance of their smiles.

  • Family dentistry
  • Cosmetic consultations & whitening
  • Minimally invasive aesthetic treatments

Sofia Martin, RDH

Registered Dental Hygienist

Sofia focuses on preventive hygiene care and helping patients build realistic, sustainable oral-health routines.

  • Dental hygiene & periodontal maintenance
  • Preventive education & home-care coaching

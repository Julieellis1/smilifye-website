# ZoeSmile Dental — Clinic Website

A complete, responsive multi-page website for **ZoeSmile Dental**, a modern, welcoming, patient-centred dental clinic in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.

All copy was written for ZoeSmile (page by page, including legal drafts), and all imagery is original AI-generated photography. The site's information architecture and art direction were inspired by the structure of the Smilifye Webflow template.

- Legal pages are drafts — have them reviewed by qualified Canadian counsel before publication.
- Verify team credentials, clinic address, phone, hours, and insurance/billing details before launch.
- Canadian spelling is used throughout (centre, personalised, etc.).

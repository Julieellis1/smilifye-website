Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

ZoeSmile Dental is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of personal information entrusted to us. This policy explains, in general terms, how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information through our website and clinic operations.

This is a draft website policy and should be reviewed by qualified Canadian privacy counsel and finalized for ZoeSmile's actual practices before publication.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Name and contact information
  • Appointment requests and scheduling information
  • Information you provide through contact forms
  • Dental and health information required to provide care
  • Insurance and billing information
  • Website and technical information such as browser type, device information, and pages visited
  • Communications and preferences where relevant

We aim to collect information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes identified.

2. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Provide and coordinate dental care
  • Communicate with patients
  • Process payments and insurance claims
  • Maintain clinical and administrative records
  • Improve our services and website
  • Meet legal, regulatory, professional, and safety obligations
  • Send optional communications where permitted and where you have provided appropriate consent

3. Health Information

Dental and health information is treated as confidential and handled in accordance with applicable privacy and healthcare requirements in British Columbia.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary for care, payment, administration, professional services, legal obligations, safety, or where you have provided consent, subject to applicable law.

Service providers may process information on our behalf where appropriate safeguards and contractual requirements apply.

5. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate properly, remember preferences, understand website usage, and improve the user experience. See our Cookies Policy for more information.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information we hold. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and as required by applicable legal, professional, and record-retention obligations.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, or information about the handling of your personal information. Requests should be directed to ZoeSmile's privacy contact.

9. Contact

10. Changes

We may update this policy when our practices, technology, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when material revisions are published.

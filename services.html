Restore Comfort, Function, and Confidence

Family Dentistry

Care for Every Generation. We welcome families and adapt appointments to each patient's age, needs, comfort level, and dental history.

Emergency Dental Care

When You Need Help Sooner. Dental pain, swelling, a broken tooth, or dental trauma can require prompt attention. Contact ZoeSmile and we will help determine the appropriate next step.

For severe facial swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, or another medical emergency, call 911.

Payment & Insurance

Making Dental Care Easier to Navigate. ZoeSmile can assist with insurance paperwork and direct-billing arrangements where available. We will discuss estimated fees and payment expectations before treatment whenever possible.

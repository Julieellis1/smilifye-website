✦ Overview

Restorative Care at ZoeSmile

Restorative treatment addresses teeth affected by decay, fractures, wear, infection, or tooth loss. Treatment depends on the condition of the tooth, your oral health, and your personal goals.

Our Process

  • Assess — we identify the cause and extent of the problem.
  • Explain — we discuss treatment options, alternatives, expected steps, and relevant considerations.
  • Treat — treatment is performed using appropriate techniques and materials.
  • Maintain — we provide guidance for caring for the restored tooth and monitoring your oral health.
✦ Treatments

Restorative Options

Tooth-Coloured Fillings

Composite fillings can restore teeth affected by cavities or small areas of damage while providing a tooth-coloured appearance.

Crowns

A crown covers and protects a significantly damaged or weakened tooth when a filling or other conservative option is not sufficient.

Bridges

A dental bridge can replace a missing tooth by using neighbouring teeth or other supporting structures, where clinically appropriate.

Root Canal Treatment

When the inner tissue of a tooth becomes inflamed or infected, root canal treatment may be recommended to remove the affected tissue and preserve the tooth.

Dental Implant Consultation

Dental implants are one option for replacing missing teeth. We assess whether implant treatment may be suitable and can coordinate appropriate care.

Tooth Repair

Chipped, cracked, or worn teeth can often be repaired conservatively. An assessment determines the most appropriate approach.

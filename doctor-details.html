Dr. Maya Chen, DDS

General Dentist & Practice Lead

About Dr. Chen

Dr. Maya Chen provides comprehensive dental care with a focus on prevention, restorative treatment, family dentistry, and helping patients make informed decisions about their oral health.

Dr. Chen believes that good dentistry starts with listening. She takes time to understand each patient's concerns, explain clinical findings in accessible language, and develop care plans that reflect their needs and priorities.

Her approach is especially focused on prevention and conservative treatment where appropriate, while also supporting patients who need more involved restorative care.

Dr. Oliver Bennett

Implant & Restorative Dentist · Founder & Clinical Director

Dr. Bennett founded Smilifye in 2004 with a simple conviction: replacing a tooth should feel as routine as filling one. Eighteen years and more than 3,000 implants later, he leads our restorative department and mentors every clinician who joins the practice.

Known for his calm chairside manner and obsessive treatment planning, he specializes in full-arch rehabilitation, bone grafting, and complex cases other clinics turn away. Every implant case is planned in 3D and reviewed with you before surgery day.

Why patients choose Dr. Bennett

  • 18 years focused exclusively on implant & restorative dentistry
  • 3,000+ implants placed with a 98.6% long-term success rate
  • Guided-surgery planning for faster healing and less discomfort
  • Sedation options for anxious patients, always judgment-free

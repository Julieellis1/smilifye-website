✦ Overview

Cosmetic Dentistry at ZoeSmile

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on the appearance of the smile. We begin with an assessment so that any aesthetic treatment is planned around healthy teeth and gums.

Our Approach

  • Natural first — results that complement your facial features and existing smile rather than a one-size-fits-all template.
  • Health before aesthetics — active dental disease or gum concerns are addressed before elective cosmetic treatment where appropriate.
  • Clear expectations — we discuss benefits, limitations, maintenance, and alternatives before you decide.
✦ Treatment Options

Options Worth Exploring

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening can lighten the appearance of natural teeth. Your dentist will assess your teeth and discuss whether whitening is appropriate and what results may reasonably be expected.

Cosmetic Bonding

Tooth-coloured composite material can be used in selected cases to improve the shape, colour, or appearance of a tooth.

Veneers

Veneers are thin restorations designed to cover the visible surface of suitable teeth. A consultation is needed to determine whether veneers are appropriate for your goals and oral health.

Smile Planning

For patients considering multiple cosmetic treatments, we can discuss priorities, sequencing, maintenance, and alternatives before treatment begins.

Clear Aligners vs. Braces: An Honest Guide for Adults

Oct 2, 2026·6 min read·Dr. Sofia Marchetti

It's the question nearly every adult patient asks at their first orthodontic consultation: "Should I get aligners or braces?" Here's the honest answer — both work beautifully, but for different people and different cases.

The case for clear aligners

Aligners are removable, nearly invisible, and let you eat whatever you like. Most adults finish treatment in 6–14 months with check-ins every 8–10 weeks instead of monthly wire adjustments. They're ideal for mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and bite issues.

The trade-off? Discipline. Aligners only work when worn 20–22 hours a day. If trays spend more time in their case than on your teeth, treatment stalls — and that's the number one reason aligner cases underperform.

The case for modern braces

Today's braces are smaller, smoother, and faster than the ones you remember. Ceramic options blend with your teeth, and self-ligating brackets can shorten treatment time. Most importantly: braces work around the clock with zero willpower required.

For complex rotations, severe bite discrepancies, or significant vertical movement, braces remain the gold standard. They give your orthodontist precise, three-dimensional control that aligners can't always match.

How we help you decide

  • A 3D scan and bite analysis — no goopy impressions
  • A digital simulation of your result with either option
  • Transparent pricing for both, including insurance estimates
  • An honest recommendation — even if that means a simpler, cheaper fix

There's no universally "better" choice — only the better choice for your teeth, your lifestyle, and your budget. That's exactly what your consultation is for.

✦ Our Story

Dentistry Should Feel Personal

ZoeSmile was imagined around a simple idea: patients deserve dental care that is clinically responsible and human at the same time.

We created our practice to give Vancouver patients a place where they can ask questions, understand their options, and receive care without feeling rushed. Our team takes time to learn what matters to each patient, from maintaining everyday oral health to addressing a specific dental concern or planning a long-term smile transformation.

✦ Our Philosophy

Listen. Explain. Care.

Listen

We start by understanding your concerns, goals, history, and priorities.

Explain

We communicate findings and treatment options in straightforward language.

Care

We provide attentive treatment and follow-up with your comfort and oral health in mind.

✦ What We Stand For

The Values Behind Every Visit

Respect

Every patient deserves to feel welcome, heard, and treated with dignity.

Prevention

We focus on helping patients identify and manage dental concerns early whenever possible.

Transparency

We discuss treatment options, expected steps, and relevant costs before proceeding.

Precision

We use appropriate modern tools and evidence-informed techniques to support diagnosis and treatment.

Continuity

We aim to build long-term relationships rather than simply provide one-off appointments.

Modern care.
Natural smiles.

Our tagline is our promise — contemporary dentistry with a human touch.

✦ Technology

Modern Tools, Human Care

Digital imaging, digital records, and other contemporary technologies can help our team assess oral health and plan treatment efficiently. Technology is never a substitute for professional judgment; we use it when it is appropriate for the patient's needs.

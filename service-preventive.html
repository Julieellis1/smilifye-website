✦ Overview

What Is Preventive Dentistry?

Preventive dentistry focuses on maintaining oral health and identifying potential problems before they become more complex. It can include examinations, professional cleanings, fluoride care, sealants, gum assessments, and personalised home-care guidance.

Why It Matters

  • Helps identify cavities and gum concerns early
  • Supports healthy gums and tooth surfaces
  • Gives you practical habits for home
  • Helps your dentist monitor changes over time
  • Can reduce the need for more extensive treatment in some cases
✦ What We Offer

Preventive Care, Explained

Dental Examinations

A clinical assessment of your teeth, gums, bite, and overall oral health, with imaging when clinically appropriate.

Professional Cleanings

Removal of plaque, calculus, and surface buildup that routine brushing cannot fully address.

Fluoride Care

Fluoride treatments may be recommended for patients who could benefit from additional protection against tooth decay.

Sealants

For suitable teeth, dental sealants can provide a protective barrier over deep grooves that are more difficult to clean.

Gum Health Monitoring

We monitor gum health and discuss appropriate home and professional care when signs of gum disease are present.

Home-Care Guidance

Practical, personalised advice on brushing, interdental cleaning, and habits that support long-term oral health.

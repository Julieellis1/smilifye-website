✦ Orthodontics

A More Confident, Comfortable Way to Straighten Teeth

Orthodontic treatment can improve tooth alignment and, in suitable cases, help address bite concerns. ZoeSmile offers assessment and clear-aligner consultations for patients who may benefit from orthodontic care.

✦ Overview

What Orthodontics Can Address

Orthodontic treatment may be used to manage crowded teeth, spacing, certain bite problems, and other alignment concerns.

A professional assessment is required to determine whether treatment is appropriate and which approach best fits your needs.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners use a sequence of removable trays to gradually move teeth according to a prescribed treatment plan. Potential advantages can include:

  • Removable trays
  • Discreet appearance
  • Digital treatment planning where appropriate
  • Easier brushing and flossing than with fixed appliances

Aligners are not suitable for every orthodontic problem, and treatment length varies.

Traditional Braces

Fixed braces use brackets and wires to apply controlled forces to move teeth. They remain an effective option for many alignment needs.

Retention

After active treatment, retainers help maintain the new tooth positions. Your clinician will explain your retention plan.

Cookies Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how ZoeSmile may use cookies and similar technologies on its website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files or identifiers stored on or associated with your device when you visit a website. They can help a website remember preferences, operate correctly, and understand how visitors use it.

How We May Use Cookies

Essential Cookies

These may be required for basic website functions, security, forms, and navigation.

Functional Cookies

These may remember preferences or settings to make the website easier to use.

Analytics Cookies

Where enabled, analytics technologies may provide aggregated information about website traffic and usage so we can improve the site.

Marketing Cookies

If ZoeSmile uses advertising or remarketing technologies, these may place or access cookies for marketing purposes, subject to applicable consent requirements.

Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where implemented, ZoeSmile's cookie preference controls.

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Third-Party Services

Some website features may rely on third-party services. Those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their policies.

Changes

We may update this policy as our website and technology change.
